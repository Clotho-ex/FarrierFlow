FarrierFlow is an iPhone-first business application for independent farriers.
This draft describes how v1.0.1 handles information when released.

## Local-first business records

FarrierFlow stores its operational business records locally on the user's
iPhone. These records include business identity, clients, horses, service
locations, appointments, visits and work performed, work notes, hoof
photographs, invoices, payment status and evidence, and next appointments.

FarrierFlow does not upload the contents of these records to its analytics or
subscription services. In particular, names, contact details, addresses,
notes, photographs, invoice contents, payment details, and local record
identifiers are not sent to PostHog or RevenueCat.

Records may leave the device only when the user deliberately uses an operating
system feature such as sharing an invoice or photograph, or when the user has
enabled a device backup or transfer service. Those actions are controlled by
the user and the applicable operating-system or destination service.

## Anonymous product analytics

FarrierFlow uses PostHog to understand whether important product workflows are
working. It sends a small set of events describing transitions such as
onboarding progress, creating an appointment, completing a visit, creating or
sharing an invoice, and changing an invoice to Paid. Subscription interaction
events may include only the selected plan type, `monthly` or `annual`.

PostHog creates, persists, and transmits a random, anonymous installation/device
identifier so events can be analyzed across sessions without a FarrierFlow
account. FarrierFlow does not read that identifier, copy it into business
records, combine it with the RevenueCat identifier, or attach names, email
addresses, phone numbers, customer identifiers, or business-record contents to
it. PostHog person profiles are disabled and FarrierFlow does not identify
users to PostHog.

PostHog automatically adds limited technical context to custom events,
including app name, bundle identifier, app version and build, PostHog SDK
version, Apple device model and type, operating-system name and version, screen
dimensions, language, time zone, Wi-Fi or cellular reachability status, and
whether the build is running through TestFlight, sideloading, Simulator, or a
Mac compatibility environment. The SDK also adds random event and session
identifiers and timestamps. It does not send the user-assigned iPhone name or
carrier name.

Events are queued on the device by the PostHog SDK when necessary for later
delivery. FarrierFlow does not maintain a separate analytics database or retry
system. Events are sent to PostHog's United States ingestion service at
`https://us.i.posthog.com`.

Each event instructs PostHog not to perform GeoIP enrichment. The PostHog
project is also configured to discard client IP data rather than retain it with
events. FarrierFlow does not use Core Location for analytics and does not send
GPS coordinates or business addresses to PostHog.

FarrierFlow disables PostHog session replay, screenshots, screen-view capture,
application-lifecycle autocapture, element-interaction autocapture, surveys,
feature-flag events and preloading, logs, and automatic error or crash capture.

## Subscriptions and purchases

FarrierFlow uses Apple's App Store and RevenueCat to offer and manage
FarrierFlow Pro subscriptions. Apple processes the purchase. RevenueCat
receives and processes App Store receipt and subscription information,
purchase history, product and entitlement status, renewals, cancellations, and
restores so FarrierFlow can determine whether Pro features are available and
so subscription performance can be analyzed.

FarrierFlow does not have an account system. RevenueCat creates a random
anonymous App User ID, caches it on the device, and uses it to process purchases
and subscription status. FarrierFlow does not assign or transmit a custom
user/account ID. It does not provide RevenueCat with a name, email address,
phone number, business identifier, client identifier, advertising identifier,
or other custom customer attribute.
The RevenueCat identifier is not sent to PostHog or stored in FarrierFlow
business records.

Apple may separately hold payment information for the user's Apple Account.
FarrierFlow and RevenueCat do not receive the user's card or bank-account
details from the App Store purchase flow.

## Apple Ads attribution

FarrierFlow enables Apple's Standard AdServices attribution through RevenueCat.
When Apple supplies attribution, RevenueCat can associate the anonymous
subscription customer with Apple Ads source, campaign, ad group, keyword, ad
attribution, and claim context for FarrierFlow's advertising/marketing and
subscription analysis.

RevenueCat's SDK requests the Apple-provided attribution token and sends it to
RevenueCat in the background. FarrierFlow does not manually fetch, decode, or
store that token, and does not send Apple Ads campaign, ad-group, keyword,
claim, or token values to PostHog or to business records.

FarrierFlow does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, access the
IDFA, use AdSupport, fingerprint a device, or implement cross-app tracking.
Standard AdServices attribution does not require the ATT prompt.

## Third-party services

The v1.0.1 candidate uses these third parties:

- **Apple** for App Store distribution, subscriptions, receipts, and Standard
  AdServices attribution. See [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **RevenueCat** for anonymous subscription identity, receipt and entitlement
  processing, subscription analytics, and Apple Ads attribution. See
  [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).
- **PostHog** for anonymous product-usage analytics in its United States
  service region. See [PostHog's Privacy Policy](https://posthog.com/privacy).

## Retention and deletion

Local business records remain on the iPhone until the user deletes individual
records or removes the app's data. Deleting the app can remove local records;
users should preserve any backup they need first.

PostHog and RevenueCat retain service data according to their service terms,
the FarrierFlow project configuration, and applicable legal requirements.
Deleting FarrierFlow removes the providers' locally cached identifiers and
queues, but does not by itself delete records already processed by those
providers or Apple. Contact FarrierFlow to request assistance with data-access
or deletion questions. Subscription and transaction records may remain where
Apple, RevenueCat, or applicable law requires them for purchase processing,
fraud prevention, accounting, or legal compliance.
