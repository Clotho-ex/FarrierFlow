## FarrierFlow's local-first model

FarrierFlow stores business records and hoof photographs locally on the device.
It does not require a FarrierFlow account and does not provide FarrierFlow cloud
sync, a developer-operated server, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

The app uses Apple's StoreKit services to offer and restore FarrierFlow Pro
subscriptions. Apple processes those App Store transactions under Apple's own
terms and privacy practices. FarrierFlow does not add a separate payment
processor or customer account.

## Your records and photographs

Business records and photographs remain on the device unless you choose an
operating-system feature that shares or backs up that device. FarrierFlow does
not transmit those records to a FarrierFlow-operated service.

The app provides explicit controls for its supported record and photograph
deletions. Deleting the app or its device data can remove locally stored
records and photographs; maintain any backup you need before doing so.

## Data collection and tracking

FarrierFlow declares no tracking and no collected data types in its app privacy
manifest. Before release, this statement must be rechecked against the final
binary and the App Store App Privacy answers.
