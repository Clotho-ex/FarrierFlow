# FarrierFlow Privacy Policy Source

**Publication status:** Published to
`https://farrierflow.vercel.app/privacy/` on 2026-08-28 and verified live with
the RevenueCat Purchase History disclosure described below.

## FarrierFlow's local-first model

FarrierFlow stores business records and hoof photographs locally on the device.
It does not require a FarrierFlow account and does not provide FarrierFlow cloud
sync, advertising, or tracking.

Apple processes FarrierFlow Pro App Store subscriptions. FarrierFlow uses
RevenueCat to validate receipts, provide subscription entitlements, show
purchase history in RevenueCat's customer/analytics tools, and restore access.
RevenueCat receives subscription Purchase History under an anonymous App User
ID. FarrierFlow does not send RevenueCat a name, email address, phone number,
advertising identifier, or other customer attribute, and adds no FarrierFlow
account or separate payment processor.

The app also lets the farrier record that a customer Invoice was paid, including
amount, date, method, optional reference, and an optional internal note. Those
business records remain local to the device and are not subscription purchase
data sent to RevenueCat.

## Your records and photographs

Business records and photographs remain on the device unless you choose an
operating-system feature that shares or backs up that device. FarrierFlow does
not transmit those records to a FarrierFlow-operated service.

The app provides explicit controls for its supported record and photograph
deletions. Deleting the app or its device data can remove locally stored
records and photographs; maintain any backup you need before doing so.

## Data collection and tracking

FarrierFlow declares no tracking. App Store Connect must disclose Purchase
History used for App Functionality and Analytics because RevenueCat processes
App Store purchase history. Under the current anonymous implementation it is
not linked to identity and is not used for tracking. Reassess these answers if
customer attributes, custom identities, integrations, advertising, or tracking
are added.
