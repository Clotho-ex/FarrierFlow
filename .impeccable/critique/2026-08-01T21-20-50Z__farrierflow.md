## Design Specificity Verdict

**Specific in workflow, generic at the highest-stakes moments.** FarrierFlow is unmistakably built around Service Locations, Horses, Visits, hoof photographs, recorded work, and Client invoices. Its calm native treatment is correct for an iPhone field tool and avoids western theming, decorative dashboards, and custom navigation.

The remaining weakness is not visual blandness. When the user must decide whether the correct work is about to become an immutable numbered invoice, the interface stops feeling farrier-specific and becomes ordinary form/list plumbing.

The bundled detector returned zero findings for `FarrierFlow`, but it has limited SwiftUI coverage. Native source review found no custom navigation, raw production colors, fixed point-size fonts, third-party controls, or main-actor PDF rendering regression. It did identify two small residual risks: a fixed 34-point alignment indent in `InvoiceVisitSelectionRow` and date strings formatted with the process locale before insertion into environment-localized accessibility labels.

## Overall Impression

FarrierFlow feels trustworthy, restrained, and substantially more inclusive after Task 6. The biggest opportunity is to turn financial commitment and blocked setup from moments of trust-me friction into moments of visible proof and direct recovery.

## What's Working

1. **The product is field-specific without costume.** Domain language and contextual creation make it recognizably FarrierFlow while standard iOS structure keeps it predictable.
2. **Failure and destructive states preserve trust.** PDF work is cancellable, Retry and Cancel are available, missing photographs remain explicit records, and Mark Paid/Delete explain their consequences.
3. **Task 6 materially improves inclusive scanning.** Appointment, Invoice, Visit-selection, and line-item rows reflow at accessibility text sizes and expose meaningful VoiceOver labels, values, and state.

## Cognitive Load

**Moderate: three checklist failures.**

- Pass: single focus, chunking, grouping, one decision at a time, and progressive disclosure.
- Fail: visual hierarchy on Invoice Creation and Invoice Detail.
- Conditional fail: eligible Horse, Visit, and Service Location selections can grow beyond four visible options without narrowing.
- Fail: users must remember Visit content, Photograph ownership/position, or setup context from another screen.

The structurally unbounded decision points are completed Visit selection in `InvoiceCreationView`, eligible Horse selection in `AppointmentEditorView`, and Service Location choices in its picker.

## Emotional Journey

The opening experience is calm and familiar. The first valley occurs when Appointment creation discovers missing setup and can only instruct the user to leave. The highest-anxiety moment is Invoice generation: an immutable financial snapshot and number will be created, but there is no combined proof of selected Visits, included work, and total. PDF sharing should be a peak, yet its progress feedback is visually quiet. Mark Paid provides the strongest ending because it clearly explains the irreversible consequence.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] Invoice generation lacks a financial preflight

**Why it matters:** A farrier can see individual Visit subtotals, but not the selected Visit count, included work-item count, or checked combined total before committing an immutable numbered Invoice. This encourages mental arithmetic and makes an avoidable billing mistake more likely.

**Fix:** Add a compact Selection Summary below the Visit choices: selected Visits, included recorded Services, and checked total. Keep it updated with selection and let it provide the reassurance a separate confirmation dialog would otherwise supply.

**Evidence:** `FarrierFlow/Features/Invoices/Views/InvoiceCreationView.swift:63`; `FarrierFlow/Features/Invoices/Components/InvoiceVisitSelectionRow.swift:38`; `InvoiceVisitChoice.eligibleWorkItemCount` already exists.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`

### [P2] Invoice Detail foregrounds administration over the decision

**Why it matters:** Users first need the Invoice identity, status, total, and next action. Business and Client contact snapshots currently occupy the first sections, while Total appears after every Visit. Share, Mark Paid, and Delete may also compete as equal toolbar actions.

**Fix:** Lead with a compact Invoice summary containing number, status, dates, and total; follow with performed work; move contact snapshots later. Keep Share and Mark Paid visible, and place eligible Delete in a native overflow menu.

**Evidence:** `FarrierFlow/Features/Invoices/Views/InvoiceDetailView.swift:42` and `:116`.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

### [P2] Appointment prerequisite recovery breaks context

**Why it matters:** When no Service Location or eligible Horse exists, the guidance is accurate but the user must cancel, navigate elsewhere, create or move data, and restart the Appointment. This is costly in one-handed field use and for first-time activation.

**Fix:** Treat this as a future workflow decision, because the approved design currently specifies explanatory guidance. If expanded, add one native contextual action and return with the prerequisite selected while preserving the Appointment draft—without introducing global workflow infrastructure.

**Evidence:** `FarrierFlow/Features/Schedule/Views/AppointmentEditorView.swift:26` and `:65`.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable onboard`

### [P2] Full-screen Photograph context is too thin

**Why it matters:** “Photograph created [date]” identifies the asset but not its Horse or its position in the collection. After interruption, and especially with VoiceOver, users cannot confidently distinguish several photographs from the same Visit.

**Fix:** Pass existing Horse name and collection position into the full-screen presentation and announce: “Hoof photograph for [Horse], [position] of [total], created [date].” Do not invent hoof-side metadata that is not stored.

**Evidence:** `FarrierFlow/Features/Photographs/Views/PhotographCollectionView.swift:89`; `FarrierFlow/Features/Photographs/Views/PhotographFullImageView.swift:78`.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

### [P3] PDF preparation feedback is visually too quiet

**Why it matters:** Replacing the Share label with a small toolbar spinner can look like the action disappeared in glare. The state is technically correct but easy to miss in the product's actual use environment.

**Fix:** Keep the toolbar state and add concise visible “Preparing PDF…” feedback in the content until the system share sheet opens. Preserve cancellation behavior.

**Evidence:** `FarrierFlow/Features/Invoices/Views/InvoiceDetailView.swift:43`.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

### Alex — experienced power user

- Select All is useful, but there is no selected count or combined total.
- Repeated same-day Visits at one location are distinguished mainly by subtotal.
- Long Visit and Horse selections have no narrowing aid.

### Sam — VoiceOver or low-vision user

- Task 6 substantially improves row order, labels, values, and large-text composition.
- Full-screen Photograph context omits Horse and collection position.
- Newly composed accessibility dates can use the process locale rather than an injected SwiftUI locale.

### Casey — distracted one-handed user

- Missing setup forces a context-breaking exit.
- The Invoice total must be calculated or remembered while scrolling.
- PDF progress is easy to overlook outdoors, although cancellation safely prevents stale files.

### Morgan — independent farrier at a busy mixed-client barn

- Several eligible Visits can share a date and Service Location without showing the included Horses or Services.
- There is no “N Visits · N Services · Total” reassurance before Generate.
- Long Horse lists increase wrong-selection risk under gloves, glare, or interruption.

## Minor Observations

- “Releases its recorded-work links” sounds internal. Prefer: “Recorded work stays in FarrierFlow and can be added to another invoice.”
- The fixed 34-point indent in `InvoiceVisitSelectionRow` couples alignment to current symbol geometry; use structural alignment if this row is touched again.
- Accessibility date labels should format dates with the same locale used for the surrounding localized string.
- Active Save-blocker guidance is accurate but visually low-emphasis; it should remain concise while carrying more hierarchy than ordinary explanatory footnotes.

## Questions to Consider

1. What proof should appear before Generate: Horse names, work-item count, combined total, or all three?
2. Is Invoice Detail primarily for verifying, sharing, or collecting payment? Its first screenful should commit to that answer.
3. Should Appointment creation continue to explain missing setup, or eventually orchestrate the minimum prerequisite and return?
4. What existing context best distinguishes several photographs: Horse name, collection position, timestamp, or all available values?
